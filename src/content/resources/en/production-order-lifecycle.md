## From proposal to order

A Planned Order from MRP becomes a Production Order once the decision to produce
is taken. At that point the order becomes an execution document.

## The stages

1. **Creation** — from a Planned Order, or directly.
2. **Release** — the order becomes executable on the shop floor.
3. **Component movements** — materials issued to the order.
4. **Confirmations** — quantities and times declared per operation.
5. **Goods receipt** — the finished product enters stock.
6. **Technical closing** — no further movements can be posted.

## What the order tells you

The gaps between planned and confirmed are worth analysing: actual times, scrap,
consumption and adherence to dates.
