## What SAP PP does in an enterprise system

SAP S/4HANA Production Planning (PP) covers the planning and execution of
production. It links commercial demand to manufacturing operations, resting on
structured master data.

## The core processes

- **Demand Management** — turn forecasts and orders into a plan.
- **MRP** — calculate component requirements and propose orders.
- **Capacity Planning** — test the plan against available capacity.
- **Production Orders** — execute, confirm and close manufacturing.

## Why it matters

An ERP only creates value once the business processes are understood. Knowing
how to navigate the screens is not enough: you need to understand what the
system is calculating, and why.
