## What the role actually demands

An ERP consultant is neither purely technical nor purely functional. The work
rests on being able to understand a business process, then translate it into
what the system can do.

## The three skills that count

- **The business process** — understand what the organization actually does.
- **The system** — know what the ERP allows, and at what cost.
- **Communication** — explain a trade-off to a non-technical audience.

## Where to start

A clear functional specialization — production, distribution, finance — is
usually more useful than a shallow familiarity with several modules.
